## Adaptive Biology
The bearer has Feel No Pain 5+. If, at the start of any turn, it has fewer wounds left than its starting number, it has Feel No Pain 4+ instead for the rest of the battle.

## Alien Cunning
After both armies deploy, redeploy up to three TYRANIDS units; they can go into Strategic Reserves regardless of the usual limit.

## Perfectly Adapted
Once per turn, re-roll one hit, wound, damage, Advance or charge roll, or one saving throw, made for the bearer.

## Synaptic Linchpin
Friendly TYRANIDS units within 9" of the bearer are within your army's Synapse Range.

## Synaptic Strategy
Once per battle, Rapid Ingress on the bearer's unit costs 0CP, even if another unit already used it this phase.

## Tremor Senses
After both armies deploy, redeploy up to three TYRANIDS units; they can go into Strategic Reserves regardless of the usual limit.

## Trygon Prime
TRYGON only. The bearer gains SYNAPSE and its melee weapons get +1 S and +1 WS.

## Vanguard Intellect
Models with Deep Strike only. The bearer's unit can arrive by Deep Strike in the Reinforcements step of your 1st, 2nd or 3rd Movement phase, regardless of mission rules.

## Power of the Hive Mind
TYRANIDS PSYKER only. +1 S and +1 AP for the bearer's psychic weapons.

## Psychostatic Disruption (Aura)
TYRANIDS SYNAPSE only. Enemy units arriving from Reserves cannot be set up within 12" of the bearer. Once per battle, in battle round 1 or 2, when your opponent says a unit will arrive from Strategic Reserves, roll D6: on a 4+ it cannot arrive this turn.

## Synaptic Control
TYRANIDS SYNAPSE only. Attacks allocated to the bearer get -1 Damage.

## The Dirgeheart of Kharis
TYRANIDS SYNAPSE only (Aura). Enemy units within 9" of the bearer get -1 Leadership.

## Biophagic Flow
While a friendly HARVESTER model is within 12" of the bearer, it can Regenerate a unit within 9" instead of 6".

## Instinctive Defence
While the bearer is within 6" of a friendly HARVESTER unit, Heroic Intervention on its unit costs 1CP less and the unit has Fights First.

## Parasitic Biomorphology
+1 S for melee weapons in the bearer's unit. The first time the unit destroys an enemy unit in the Fight phase while the bearer is within 6" of a friendly HARVESTER, its melee weapons get +1 A for the rest of the battle.

## Regenerating Monstrosity
Not MONSTER models. The bearer's unit can be regenerated up to twice per phase.

## Enraged Reserves
TYRANIDS MONSTER only. If the bearer is destroyed by a melee attack before it has fought this phase, roll D6: on a 3+ it can fight after the attacking unit finishes, then is removed.

## Monstrous Nemesis
TYRANIDS MONSTER only. +1 to wound for the bearer's melee attacks against MONSTER or VEHICLE units.

## Null Nodules
TYRANIDS MONSTER only. Once per battle, when a psychic attack is allocated to the bearer, it has Feel No Pain 5+ against psychic attacks until the end of the phase.

## Ominous Presence
TYRANIDS MONSTER model only. Add 3 to the Objective Control characteristic of the bearer.

## Chameleonic
VANGUARD INVADER only. The bearer's unit has Stealth.

## Hunting Grounds
While the bearer is on the battlefield, each time your opponent sets up a unit from Reserves, roll D6: on a 2+ that unit takes a battle-shock test.

## Neuronode
After both armies deploy, redeploy up to three VANGUARD INVADER units; any of them can go into Strategic Reserves regardless of the usual limit.

## Stalker
VANGUARD INVADER only. At the start of the battle pick one enemy unit: the bearer gets +1 to hit and +1 to wound against it.

## Adrenalised Onslaught
The bearer's unit can pile in and consolidate 3" further.

## Naturalised Camouflage
Start of the first battle round: pick up to three friendly ENDLESS MULTITUDE units within 9" of the bearer. Until the end of that round they have the benefit of cover against ranged attacks.

## Piercing Talons
Attacks by the bearer's unit get +1 AP on a critical wound.

## Relentless Hunger
Add 2" to the Move characteristic of models in the bearer's unit.

## Cryptophotaic Camouflage
VON RYAN'S LEAPERS only. The unit has -3" detection range.

## Encircling Horrors
NEUROLICTOR, LICTOR or VON RYAN'S LEAPERS only. In your opponent's Movement phase, when an enemy unit ends a move within 8", this unit can make a Normal move of up to D3+3".

## Destabilising Predation
NORN EMISSARY only. Its ranged attacks have [ANTI-CHARACTER 2+].

## Synaptoprescience
NORN ASSIMILATOR only. It has a 4+ invulnerable save.

## Elevated Might
WINGED TYRANID PRIME or TYRANID PRIME WITH LASH WHIP only. The bearer's melee attacks can re-roll wound rolls and get +1 AP.

## Ocular Adaptation
WINGED TYRANID PRIME or TYRANID PRIME WITH LASH WHIP only. Melee attacks by the bearer's unit get +1 to hit.
