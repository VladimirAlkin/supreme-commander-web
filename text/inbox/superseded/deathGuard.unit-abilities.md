## Mortarion :: Supreme Commander
If this model is in your army, it must be your WARLORD.

## Mortarion :: Host of Plagues
End of your Movement phase: roll D6 for each enemy unit within 6" (+1 if it is Afflicted). On a 3+ it suffers D3 mortal wounds.

## Mortarion :: Lord of the Death Guard
Once per turn Mortarion can use one of these: Diseased Influence - after an enemy unit ends a Normal, Advance or Fall Back move within 8" of a friendly unengaged DEATH GUARD unit within 6" of him, that unit can make a Normal move of up to 5". Boon of Death - in the Fight phase, when a friendly DEATH GUARD unit within 6" is targeted, until the end of the phase its models destroyed by melee attacks before fighting roll D6: on a 2+ they fight after the attacker, then are removed. Inflamed Reprisal - in your opponent's Shooting phase, when a friendly DEATH GUARD unit within 6" is targeted, after the attacker finishes, that unit can shoot back at it as if it were your Shooting phase, with -1 BS.

## Typhus :: The Destroyer Hive
While this model leads a unit, melee attacks against that unit get -1 to hit.

## Typhus :: Eater Plague (Psychic)
In your Shooting phase, you can select one enemy unit within 18" of and visible to this model and roll one D6: on a 1, this model's unit suffers D3 mortal wounds; on a 2-5, that enemy unit suffers D3 mortal wounds; on a 6, that enemy unit suffers D6 mortal wounds.

## Daemon Prince of Nurgle :: Death Guard Defenders
While within 3" of a friendly DEATH GUARD INFANTRY unit, this model has Lone Operative.

## Daemon Prince of Nurgle :: Fevered Strategist
Once per battle round (one model with this ability per army): when a friendly DEATH GUARD unit within 12" is targeted with a Stratagem, that use costs 1CP less.

## Daemon Prince of Nurgle :: Miasma of Pestilence (Aura)
Friendly DEATH GUARD units within 6" have the benefit of cover against ranged attacks.

## Daemon Prince of Nurgle with Wings :: Horrifying Visage
Each time this model ends a Charge move, pick one enemy unit engaged with it: that unit takes a battle-shock test at -1.

## Daemon Prince of Nurgle with Wings :: Enfeebling Miasma (Aura)
Enemy units (not MONSTERS or VEHICLES) within 6" must take Desperate Escape tests whenever they fall back, at -1 if they are battle-shocked.

## Lord of Contagion :: Vector of Disease
While this model leads a unit, melee weapons in that unit have [SUSTAINED HITS 1] and [LANCE].

## Lord of Contagion :: Unholy Resilience
The first time this model is destroyed in a battle round, roll D6 at the end of the phase: on a 2+ set it back up as close as possible to where it died (not in Engagement Range) with 3 wounds. Once per battle.

## Lord of Virulence :: Virulent Aura
While this model leads a unit, ranged attacks by that unit can re-roll the wound roll.

## Lord of Virulence :: Blight Bombardment
Start of your Shooting phase: pick one visible enemy unit within 30". Until the end of the phase, friendly DEATH GUARD ranged attacks against it re-roll hit rolls of 1 (Blast weapons can re-roll the hit roll).

## Lord of Poxes :: Gift of Poxes
This model's Contagion Range is 3" larger.

## Lord of Poxes :: Shroud of Disease
While this model leads a unit, that unit can only be targeted by ranged attacks from models within 18".

## Malignant Plaguecaster :: Gift of Contagion (Psychic)
While this model leads a unit, that unit's attacks against Afflicted units have [SUSTAINED HITS 1].

## Malignant Plaguecaster :: Pestilent Fallout (Psychic)
Your Shooting phase, after this model shoots: pick one enemy INFANTRY unit hit by its Plague Wind. Until the end of your opponent's next turn it is enfeebled: -2" Move.

## Noxious Blightbringer :: Sickening Vitality
While this model leads a unit, that unit gets +1" Move and can re-roll Advance and Charge rolls.

## Noxious Blightbringer :: Tocsin of Misery (Aura)
Battle-shock step of your opponent's Command phase: each enemy unit below Starting Strength within 9" takes a battle-shock test (-1 if it is a PSYKER unit).

## Foul Blightspawn :: Putrefying Stink
Enemy models cannot start or end an Advance move within 9" of this model.

## Foul Blightspawn :: Blinding Spray
Fight phase: one model with this ability per army can give its unit Fights First until the end of the phase. Each model once per battle.

## Biologus Putrifier :: Extraction of Fresh Disease
The first time this model's unit destroys an enemy unit with a melee attack, this model gets +6 OC for the rest of the battle.

## Biologus Putrifier :: Foul Infusion
While this model leads a unit, that unit's weapons have [LETHAL HITS] and score critical hits on unmodified hit rolls of 5+.

## Tallyman :: Malicious Calculations
While this model leads a unit, that unit's attacks can ignore any or all modifiers to BS/WS and to the hit roll.

## Tallyman :: Sevenfold Chant
Your Command phase: if this model is on the battlefield, roll 2D6. On 7+ you gain 1CP.

## Plague Surgeon :: Tainted Narthecium
While this model leads a unit, in your Command phase you can return 1 destroyed Bodyguard model to that unit.

## Plague Surgeon :: Inflamed Infections
Until the end of the phase, each time this model makes an attack that targets that unit, an unmodified Hit roll of 5+ scores a Critical Hit.

## Icon Bearer :: Unclean Icon
While this model leads a unit, models in that unit get +1 OC.

## Icon Bearer :: Blessed Icon of Disease
Once per battle, at the start of any phase: one friendly battle-shocked DEATH GUARD unit within 12" stops being battle-shocked.

## Plague Marines :: Infused with the Blessings of Nurgle
Your Shooting phase, after this unit shoots: pick one enemy unit it hit. It is Afflicted until the start of your next turn.

## Plague Marines :: Icon of Despair (Aura)
Enemy units within 6" of the bearer get -1 Leadership.

## Poxwalkers :: Curse of the Walking Pox
Each time a Poxwalker destroys an enemy model (not MONSTER or VEHICLE), after the unit's attacks you can return one destroyed Poxwalker to the unit. While TYPHUS leads the unit, models killed by his Eater Plague count too.

## Blightlord Terminators :: Blistering Fusillade
Each time a model in this unit makes a ranged attack, improve the Strength and Armour Penetration characteristics of that attack by 1.

## Deathshroud Terminators :: Silent Bodyguard
While a CHARACTER leads this unit, that CHARACTER has Feel No Pain 4+.

## Deathshroud Terminators :: Death Approaches
In your Movement phase, when this unit arrives by Deep Strike it can be set up more than 6" horizontally from Afflicted enemy units (and more than 8" from other enemy units).

## Deathshroud Terminators :: Icon of Despair (Aura)
Enemy units within 6" of the bearer get -1 Leadership.

## Chaos Spawn :: Lethal Ichor
Each time a melee attack is allocated to a model in this unit, after the attacking unit finishes, roll D6 (max six per attacking unit): each 4+ inflicts 1 mortal wound on the attacking unit.

## Helbrute :: Diseased Malice
Attacks by this model against Afflicted units get +1 to wound.

## Helbrute :: Froth-spattered Frenzy
If equipped with 2 melee weapons besides its close combat weapon, those two weapons get +2 A.

## Myphitic Blight-haulers :: Tank Hunters
Your Shooting phase: attacks against MONSTER or VEHICLE units get +1 to hit and +1 to wound.

## Foetid Bloat-drone :: Hovering Death
This model can shoot and declare a charge in a turn in which it fell back.

## Foetid Bloat-drone with Heavy Blight Launcher :: Explosive Blight
Your Shooting phase: when this model's attack destroys an enemy unit, roll D6 before removing its last model (+1 if it was Afflicted). On a 5+ every enemy unit within 6" of that model is Afflicted until the start of your next turn.

## Plagueburst Crawler :: Spore-laced Shock Waves
After resolving all of this model's attacks against the target unit, each unit struck by spores suffers D3 mortal wounds.

## Chaos Land Raider :: Assault Ramp
A unit that disembarks after this model made a Normal move makes an assault disembark move.

## Chaos Predator Annihilator :: Metalophagic Infection
Your Shooting phase, after this model shoots: pick one MONSTER or VEHICLE unit it hit and roll D6 (+1 if Afflicted). On a 5+ it suffers D3 mortal wounds.

## Chaos Predator Destructor :: Hail of Corrosive Disease
Your Shooting phase, after this model shoots: pick one enemy unit (not MONSTER or VEHICLE) it hit. Until the end of the phase, friendly DEATH GUARD ranged attacks against it get +1 AP (once per phase per enemy unit).

## Defiler :: Scuttling Walker
On Normal, Advance and Fall Back moves it can move through models (not TITANIC) and terrain, crossing Engagement Range without ending there; it automatically passes Desperate Escape tests.

## Defiler :: Barrage of Filth

## Chaos Rhino :: Fire Support
Your Shooting phase, after this model shoots: pick one enemy unit it hit. Until the end of the phase, models that disembarked from it this turn can re-roll wound rolls against that unit.

## Miasmic Malignifier :: Putrescent Fog (Aura)
Enemy units arriving as Reinforcements cannot be set up within 12" of this model.

## Miasmic Malignifier :: Diseased Cover
A model that is not fully visible to the attacking unit because of this Fortification has the benefit of cover against that ranged attack.

## Miasmic Malignifier :: Deployment
Deploy both parts within 1" of each other; together they count as a single model.

## Miasmic Malignifier :: Fortification
While an enemy unit is only within Engagement Range of your FORTIFICATIONS, it can still be shot (non-Pistol attacks get -1 to hit), and its models do not take Desperate Escape tests for falling back while battle-shocked (unless moving over enemy models).

## Rotigus :: Deluge of Nurgle (Aura)
