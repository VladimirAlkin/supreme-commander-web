## Saintly Example
ADEPTA SORORITAS model only. When the bearer is destroyed, you gain D3 extra Miracle dice (an Imagifier within 12" can re-roll them).

## Through Suffering, Strength
ADEPTA SORORITAS model only. Add 1 to the Attacks, Strength and Damage characteristics of the bearer’s melee weapons. If the bearer has lost one or more wounds, add 2 to the Attacks, Strength and Damage characteristics of the bearer’s melee weapons instead.

## Chaplet of Sacrifice
ADEPTA SORORITAS model only. At the end of your Command phase, if the bearer is on the battlefield, you can re-roll 1 Miracle dice from your Miracle dice pool and return it to your Miracle dice pool showing the new result you rolled. When doing so, if the bearer's unit is below its Starting Strength, you can re-roll up to 3 Miracle dice in this way instead.

## Mantle of Ophelia
CANONESS or PALATINE model only. Each time an attack is allocated to the bearer, change the Damage characteristic of that attack to 1.

## Psalm of Righteous Judgement
ADEPTA SORORITAS model only. While it is on the battlefield, each time a PENITENT unit of yours destroys an enemy unit you can discard 1 Miracle dice to gain one showing 6.

## Verse of Holy Piety
PENITENT model only. Once per battle, at the start of the battle round, pick a Vow (even one already used): it is also active for the bearer's unit until the next battle round.

## Refrain of Enduring Faith
PENITENT model only. While it leads a unit, its models have a 5+ invulnerable save.

## Catechism of Divine Penitence
CANONESS, PALATINE or MINISTORUM PRIEST only. The bearer gains PENITENT and can lead a REPENTIA SQUAD.

## Righteous Rage
ADEPTA SORORITAS model only. When the bearer is selected to fight, discard up to 3 Miracle dice: +1 A and +1 S on its melee weapons per die until the end of the phase.

## Manual of Saint Griselda
ADEPTA SORORITAS model only. Start of your Command phase: discard up to 2 Miracle dice and gain one whose value is their sum (max 6).

## Fire and Fury
ADEPTA SORORITAS model only. While it leads a unit: +1 A for its Torrent weapons, and its other ranged weapons have [SUSTAINED HITS 1].

## Iron Surplice of Saint Istalela
CANONESS or PALATINE only. Save 2+ and Feel No Pain 5+.

## Litanies of Faith
CANONESS or PALATINE only. Start of your Command phase, if on the battlefield: take a Leadership test; if passed, gain 1 Miracle dice.

## Blade of Saint Ellynor
ADEPTA SORORITAS model only. +1 S and +1 AP on the bearer's melee weapons, which have [PRECISION]; each time it fights and kills one or more models, gain 1 Miracle dice.

## Divine Aspect
ADEPTA SORORITAS model only. Your Movement phase: one enemy unit within 12" takes a Battle-shock test; if it fails, gain 1 Miracle dice.

## Triptych of the Macharian Crusade
ADEPTA SORORITAS model only. A saving throw the bearer replaces with a Miracle dice always succeeds, whatever its value.

## Triptych of Judgement
ADEPTA SORORITAS model only. Attacks by the bearer's unit ignore modifiers to BS/WS and to the hit roll.

## Mark of Devotion
ADEPTA SORORITAS model only. +1 A on the bearer's melee weapons; while its unit is Righteous, +2 A and +1 D instead.

## Eyes of the Oracle
ADEPTA SORORITAS model only. The bearer's weapons have [PRECISION]; each time its unit destroys an enemy CHARACTER model, gain 1CP.

## Sanctified Amulet
ADEPTA SORORITAS model only. Enemy units arriving from Reserves cannot be set up within 12" of the bearer.

## Clarion of Urgency
CANONESS WITH JUMP PACK only. End of your opponent's Fight phase, if unengaged: place its unit into Strategic Reserves.

## Symphonic Payload
EXORCIST unit only. It can re-roll rolls for the number of attacks of its weapons.

## Writ of Compunction
CELESTIAN SACRESANTS unit only. Add 1 to the Objective Control characteristic of models in the bearer's unit.

## Perfervid Haste
ADEPTA SORORITAS model only. The bearer's unit has +1" Move.

## Hagiomnifex
ADEPTA SORORITAS CHARACTER only (not PENITENT). Once per turn, at the start of a phase, pick one for the bearer's unit until the end of the phase: enemy units have +6" detection range while it shoots; it passes Battle-shock automatically; +1" Move; +1 S on its attacks; or attacks with S above its T get -1 to wound.
