## Lord of Forbidden Lore
THOUSAND SONS model only. Rituals the bearer manifests get +6" range.

## Incandaeum
EXALTED SORCERER only. Once per battle the bearer can attempt Doombolt even if another model already attempted it this phase.

## Umbralefic Crystal
THOUSAND SONS model only. Once per battle (per army), in your Command phase, if unengaged: place the unit in Strategic Reserves; it has Deep Strike until your next Shooting phase and must make an ingress move in your next Movement phase (including in your first turn).

## Eldritch Vortex of E'Taph
THOUSAND SONS model only. +1 S and +1 D for the bearer's Psychic weapons.

## Nethershriek Mind-Eater
THOUSAND SONS or LORD OF CHANGE model only. Start of your Shooting phase: one visible enemy unit within 12" takes a battle-shock test; if failed it suffers 3 mortal wounds.

## Diabolic Savant
THOUSAND SONS INFANTRY model only. While within 6" of a friendly SCINTILLATING LEGIONS unit, +1 to its Psychic test result when it Channels the Warp.

## Duplicitous Malediction
THOUSAND SONS or LORD OF CHANGE model only. After both armies deploy, redeploy up to three THOUSAND SONS units; they can go into Strategic Reserves regardless of the usual limit.

## Tome of True Names
THOUSAND SONS INFANTRY model only. Once per battle, at the start of any phase: the bearer has a 2+ invulnerable save until the end of the phase.

## Warpmeld Dagger
TZAANGOR SHAMAN only. When it attempts a Ritual, before the result it can suffer D3 mortal wounds; if it survives, add that many to the Psychic test result.

## Diamond of Distortion
TZAANGOR SHAMAN only. While it leads a unit, attacks against that unit get -1 to hit.

## Bray Lord
SORCERER or INFERNAL MASTER only. The bearer has Scouts 6" and can lead TZAANGORS.

## Flowing Flesh
TZAANGOR SHAMAN only. The bearer has the Feel No Pain 4+ ability and a Wounds characteristic of 5.

## Risen Rubricae
THOUSAND SONS model only. At the start of Declare Battle Formations pick two RUBRICAE BATTLELINE units or one other RUBRICAE unit: they have Infiltrators (an attached CHARACTER too).

## Arcane Thralls (Aura)
THOUSAND SONS model only. Friendly RUBRICAE units within 9" can re-roll battle-shock tests.

## Lord of the Rubricae
THOUSAND SONS model only. While the bearer leads a unit, attacks by RUBRICAE models in it get +1 to hit.

## The Stave Abominus
THOUSAND SONS INFANTRY model only. The bearer's melee weapons have [SUSTAINED HITS D3] and [DEVASTATING WOUNDS].

## Warp Syphon
Each time the bearer Channels the Warp, the bearer can use this Enhancement. If it does, select one friendly THOUSAND SONS VEHICLE unit within 6"; that VEHICLE unit suffers 1 mortal wound and you can re-roll that additional D6.

## The Perplexing Cloak
THOUSAND SONS INFANTRY model only. Lone Operative while within 3" of a friendly THOUSAND SONS VEHICLE.

## Biomechanical Mutation
THOUSAND SONS model only. Your Command phase: one friendly THOUSAND SONS VEHICLE model within 6" regains up to D3 wounds.

## Warp-Cursed Runemaster
THOUSAND SONS model only. While within 6" of a friendly THOUSAND SONS VEHICLE, Rituals it manifests get +6" range.

## Eruption of Vitality
INFANTRY/MOUNTED THOUSAND SONS PSYKER only. Once per battle (per army), when it is destroyed, roll D6 at the end of the phase: on a 2+ set it back up unengaged as close as possible, on its own, with 3 wounds. This model is not part of an attached unit and its unit has a starting strength of 1.

## Curse of Life
INFANTRY/MOUNTED THOUSAND SONS PSYKER only. When it heals through Sorcerous Invigoration, it can heal 3 more wounds.

## Walking Rampart
SORCERER or EXALTED SORCERER only. Movement phase, start or end of its move: one SEKHETAR ROBOTS unit within 3" heals D3+1 wounds. Lone Operative while within 3" of a SEKHETAR ROBOTS unit.

## Occulus Infernum
SORCERER or EXALTED SORCERER only. Movement phase, start or end of its move: one SEKHETAR ROBOTS unit within 6" gets +1 BS for ranged attacks until your next turn.

## Unravelled Fates
TZAANGOR SHAMAN only. Movement phase, start or end of its move: one battle-shocked friendly MUTANT unit within 6" is no longer battle-shocked.

## Thicket of Bladed Bone
SPAWN unit only. Its melee attacks get +1 AP and [CLEAVE 1].

## Arcane Might
THOUSAND SONS model only. +1 S for Psychic weapons in the bearer's unit (+2 while wholly within your Flow of Magic).

## Empowered Manifestation
THOUSAND SONS model only. While wholly within your Flow of Magic: +6" to the range of its ranged Psychic abilities (Rituals too) and re-roll Hazardous tests for Psychic weapons.

## Empyric Onslaught
THOUSAND SONS model only. While wholly within your Flow of Magic: +3 A for the bearer's ranged Psychic weapons.

## Noctilith Mantle
THOUSAND SONS model only. The bearer's unit is always wholly within your Flow of Magic, but its models cannot attempt Rituals.
