## Magnus the Red :: Supreme Commander
If this model is in your army, it must be your WARLORD.

## Magnus the Red :: Unearthly Power
Start of each battle round: Magnus gains one Crimson King ability until the next round. Impossible Form: -1 Damage for non-psychic attacks against him. Treason of Tzeentch: start of your opponent's Shooting phase, one enemy unit within 24" has [HAZARDOUS] ranged weapons that phase. Time Flux (Aura): friendly THOUSAND SONS units within 6" get +2" Move.

## Magnus the Red :: Lord of the Planet of the Sorcerers (Psychic)
Magnus can attempt up to two Rituals per turn and adds 2 to his Psychic test results.

## Ahriman :: Scryer of Fates (Psychic)
After both armies deploy, redeploy up to three THOUSAND SONS units; they can go into Strategic Reserves regardless of the usual limit.

## Ahriman :: Arch-Sorcerer of Tzeentch (Psychic)
+1 to this model's Psychic test results.

## Exalted Sorcerer :: Arcane Shield (Psychic)
While this model leads a unit, its models have a 4+ invulnerable save.

## Exalted Sorcerer :: Rebind Rubricae (Psychic)
In your Command phase, if this model is leading a unit, you can roll one D6: on a 1, that unit suffers D3 mortal wounds; on a 2-5, you can return up to D3 destroyed Bodyguard models to that unit; on a 6, you can return up to 3 destroyed Bodyguard models to that unit.

## Exalted Sorcerer on Disc of Tzeentch :: Illusions of Tzeentch (Psychic)
While this model leads a unit, that unit can only be shot by models within 18".

## Exalted Sorcerer on Disc of Tzeentch :: Binding Tendrils (Psychic)
Your Shooting phase, after this model shoots: one enemy INFANTRY unit hit by its Arcane Fire is ensnared until your next turn: -2" Move and -2 to charge rolls.

## Infernal Master :: Malefic Maelstrom (Psychic)
While this model leads a unit, weapons in that unit have [SUSTAINED HITS 1].

## Infernal Master :: Glimpse of Eternity (Psychic)
Once per turn, change one hit roll, wound roll or saving throw made for this model to an unmodified 6.

## Sorcerer :: Twisted Sorceries (Psychic)
Once per battle, in your Shooting phase or the Fight phase: +3 S and +3 A for this model's Psychic weapons until the end of the phase.

## Sorcerer :: Empyric Guidance (Psychic)
While this model leads a unit, weapons in that unit have [LETHAL HITS].

## Sorcerer in Terminator Armour :: Marked by Fate (Psychic)
Start of your Shooting phase: pick one enemy unit visible to this model. Until the end of the phase, attacks by models in this unit against it get +1 to hit.

## Sorcerer in Terminator Armour :: Empyric Guidance (Psychic)
While this model leads a unit, weapons in that unit have [LETHAL HITS].

## Daemon Prince of Tzeentch :: Spirit Snare
When a friendly THOUSAND SONS PSYKER with Cabal of Sorcerers dies within 9" of models with this ability, one of them gets +1 to its Psychic tests for the rest of the battle (max +2).

## Daemon Prince of Tzeentch :: Servile Pawns
While within 3" of a friendly THOUSAND SONS INFANTRY unit, this model has Lone Operative.

## Daemon Prince of Tzeentch :: Glamour of Tzeentch (Aura, Psychic)
Friendly THOUSAND SONS INFANTRY units within 6" have Stealth.

## Daemon Prince of Tzeentch with Wings :: Aetherstride (Psychic)
When it arrives by Deep Strike in your Movement phase it can aetherstride: set up more than 6" horizontally away from enemy units, its Dark Blessing gains [SUSTAINED HITS D3] this turn, and it cannot charge this turn.

## Daemon Prince of Tzeentch with Wings :: Hunter of Souls
Attacks against CHARACTER units re-roll hit and wound rolls of 1 (full re-rolls against PSYKER CHARACTERS). Destroying a CHARACTER unit heals up to D3 wounds (3 if it was a PSYKER).

## Tzaangor Shaman :: Sacrificial Blessing
In your Shooting phase and the Fight phase, each time that unit is selected to shoot or fight, this model can use this ability. If it does, destroy one Bodyguard model in that unit; until the end of the phase, add D3 to the Attacks and Strength characteristics of weapons equipped by models in that unit.

## Tzaangor Shaman :: Bestial Prophet
While this model leads a unit, that unit's attacks get +1 to hit.

## Rubric Marines :: Bringers of Change
Ranged attacks re-roll wound rolls of 1, or the whole wound roll against a unit within range of an objective you do not control.

## Rubric Marines :: Icon of Flame
Ranged weapons of the bearer's unit (not CHARACTERS) have [IGNORES COVER].

## Scarab Occult Terminators :: Rites of Coalescence
While this unit contains a PSYKER model, attacks against it get -1 to wound.

## Tzaangors :: Ambushing Hunters
End of your opponent's turn: if more than 6" from all enemy units, you can place this unit into Strategic Reserves.

## Tzaangors :: Brayhorn
Re-roll Advance and charge rolls for the bearer's unit.

## Tzaangors :: Herd banner
While the bearer's unit is within range of an objective you control, +1 Leadership (better) for its models.

## Tzaangor Enlightened :: Prophesied Doom
Each time this unit ends a Charge move, pick one engaged enemy unit and roll D6 per model in range: each 4+ inflicts 1 mortal wound.

## Tzaangor Enlightened with Fatecaster Greatbows :: Malign Trickery
In your opponent's Movement phase, when an enemy unit ends a move within 8" and this unit is unengaged, it can make a Normal move of up to D6".

## Mutalith Vortex Beast :: Mutating Vortex (Aura)
End of your Movement phase: roll D6 for each enemy unit within 6": 2-3 = 1 mortal wound, 4-5 = D3, 6 = D6; each of those units then takes a battle-shock test.

## Mutalith Vortex Beast :: Immaterial Flare (Aura)
A friendly THOUSAND SONS PSYKER within 6" adds 1 to its Psychic test result when it Channels the Warp (does not stack with other modifiers).

## Chaos Spawn :: Regenerating Monstrosities
At the start of each player's Command phase, one model in this unit regains up to 3 wounds.

## Sekhetar Robots :: Prophetic Sentinels
Once per turn, Fire Overwatch or Heroic Intervention on this unit costs 1CP less.

## Chaos Rhino :: Sorcerous Support
Your Shooting phase, after this model shoots: pick one enemy unit it hit. Until the end of the phase, Psychic Attacks against it by models that disembarked from this transport this turn get +1 to hit and +1 to wound.

## Chaos Land Raider :: Assault Ramp
A unit that disembarks after this model made a Normal move makes an assault disembark move.

## Chaos Predator Annihilator :: Ensorcelled Annihilation
Ranged attacks against a MONSTER or VEHICLE unit already hit this phase by a THOUSAND SONS PSYKER's Psychic Attack (Doombolt included) can re-roll the hit roll and the damage roll.

## Chaos Predator Destructor :: Ensorcelled Destruction
Ranged attacks against a non-MONSTER, non-VEHICLE unit already hit this phase by a THOUSAND SONS PSYKER's Psychic Attack (Doombolt included) get +1 S and +1 AP.

## Chaos Vindicator :: Siege Shield
Its demolisher cannon can target enemy units in Engagement Range with it (if no other friendly unit is engaged with them), and it suffers no hit penalty for shooting while engaged.

## Defiler :: Scuttling Walker
On Normal, Advance and Fall Back moves it can move through models (not TITANIC) and terrain, crossing Engagement Range without ending there; it automatically passes Desperate Escape tests.

## Defiler :: Destroyer of Futures (Once per phase, per unit)
Once per phase you can use Counteroffensive on this unit even if it was already used this phase; that use costs 1CP less and does not stop other units using it.

## Forgefiend :: Blazing Salvos
Your Shooting phase, after this model shoots: one enemy unit it hit is suppressed until your next turn: -1 to hit for its attacks.

## Maulerfiend :: Snarling Protector
Heroic Intervention on this unit costs 1CP less and ignores other uses this phase. When it declares a charge with a friendly engaged PSYKER unit within 12", it can re-roll the charge but must end engaged with an enemy unit engaged with that PSYKER.

## Helbrute :: Terrifying Assault
In your Shooting phase and the Fight phase, after this model has shot or fought, select one enemy unit hit by one or more of those attacks; that enemy unit must take a Battle-shock test.

## Helbrute :: Devoted to Destruction
If equipped with 2 melee weapons besides its close combat weapon, those two weapons get +2 A.

## Heldrake :: Flame-wreathed
Each time this model ends a Normal move, one enemy unit it moved over cannot have the benefit of cover until the end of the turn.

## Lord of Change :: Daemon Lord of Tzeentch (Aura)

## Lord of Change :: Master of Magicks (Psychic)
Your Shooting phase: its Bolt of Change gains [IGNORES COVER], [LETHAL HITS] or [SUSTAINED HITS D3] (your pick) until the end of the phase.

## Kairos Fateweaver :: One Head Looks Forward
End of your Command phase: if this model is on the battlefield, take a Leadership test; if passed, gain 1CP.

## Kairos Fateweaver :: One Head Looks Back (Aura)
Once per turn, when your opponent targets one of their units within 12" with a Stratagem, you can make that use cost 1CP more.

## Pink Horrors :: Split

## Pink Horrors :: Daemonic Icon

## Pink Horrors :: Instrument of Chaos
+1 to charge rolls for the bearer's unit.

## Pink Horrors :: Horrors are Pink. Horrors are Blue. Whereonce there was one, now there are two.
If, at any point, this unit contains no PINK HORROR models, use the BLUE HORRORS datasheet for this unit. While this unit contains one or more PINK HORROR models, the Sullen Malevolence and Exploding Horrors abilities from the BLUE HORRORS datasheet do not apply to this unit.

## Blue Horrors :: Split
When a Horror in this unit is destroyed, after the attacker finishes (if the unit survives) roll D6: on a 4+ a Pink Horror becomes two Blue Horrors, a Blue Horror becomes one Brimstone Horror.

## Blue Horrors :: Sullen Malevolence (Aura)
While an enemy unit is within 6" of this unit, worsen the Leadership characteristic of models in that enemy unit by 1.

## Blue Horrors :: Exploding Horrors
Each time a BRIMSTONE HORROR model in this unit is destroyed, you can select one enemy unit within Engagement Range of it, then select one or more BRIMSTONE HORROR models in this unit that were destroyed this phase; for each model selected, roll one D6: on a 4+, that enemy unit suffers 1 mortal wound.

## Flamers :: Bounding Leaps
This unit can shoot in a turn in which it fell back.

## Screamers :: Slashing Dive
Your Movement phase, after a Normal move: pick one enemy unit it moved over and roll D6 per model: each 4+ inflicts 1 mortal wound.
