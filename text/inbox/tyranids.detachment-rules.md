## Hyper-adaptations
At the start of the first battle round, select one of the following Hyper-adaptations to be active for TYRANIDS units from your army until the end of the battle: Swarming Instincts – Each time a TYRANIDS model with this Hyper-adaptation makes an attack that targets an INFANTRY or SWARM unit, that attack has the [SUSTAINED HITS 1] ability. Hyper-aggression – Each time a TYRANIDS model with this Hyper-adaptation makes an attack that targets a MONSTER or VEHICLE unit, that attack has the [LETHAL HITS] ability. Hive Predators – Each time a TYRANIDS model with this Hyper-adaptation makes an attack that targets a CHARACTER unit, on a Critical Hit, that attack has the [PRECISION] ability.

## Surprise Assault
Each time a VANGUARD INVADER unit from your army is set up on the battlefield from Reserves or Strategic Reserves, until the end of the turn, each time a model in that unit makes an attack that targets an enemy unit that is within 9", add 1 to the Hit roll.

## Synaptic Imperatives
At the start of the battle round, you can select one of the Synaptic Imperatives shown below to be active for your army until the end of the battle round. You can only select each Synaptic Imperative once per battle.

## Feed the Swarm
In your Command phase, each HARVESTER unit from your army can Regenerate one friendly TYRANIDS unit within 6". To do so, that HARVESTER unit is not eligible to shoot this turn, and you return up to D3 destroyed models (excluding CHARACTER models) to that TYRANIDS unit.

## Enraged Behemoths
Each time a TYRANIDS MONSTER model from your army makes an attack, add 1 to the Hit roll if that model’s unit is below its Starting Strength, and add 1 to the Wound roll as well if that model’s unit is Below Half-strength.

## Questing Tendrils
While the bearer is on the battlefield, each time your opponent sets up a Reserves unit on the battlefield, if that unit is set up within 12" of the bearer and more than 9" away from all other friendly TYRANIDS units from your army, that unit must take a Battle-shock test.

## Insurmountable Odds
In your opponent’s Shooting phase, when an enemy unit has shot, if a model from a friendly ENDLESS MULTITUDE unit was destroyed as a result of those attacks and that ENDLESS MULTITUDE unit is not Battle-shocked, that unit can make a surge move.

## Mindhunger
Friendly DEATHLEAPER, LICTOR and NEUROLICTOR units have the Deep Strike ability. Friendly LICTOR and NEUROLICTOR units have the Lone Operative ability. Each time a friendly DEATHLEAPER, LICTOR, NEUROLICTOR or VON RYAN’S LEAPERS unit from your army makes an attack that targets an enemy unit that is Battle-shocked, you can re-roll the Hit roll and you can re-roll the Wound roll.

## Higher Imperatives
Friendly NORN EMISSARY and NORN ASSIMILATOR units have the following ability: Protean Purpose: Once per battle, at the start of your Command phase, you can select one of the Synaptic Imperatives. Until the end of the battle round, that Synaptic Imperative is active for your army in addition to any other that is currently active.

## Leader-beasts
Friendly TYRANID WARRIORS WITH RANGED BIO-WEAPONS, TYRANID WARRIORS WITH MELEE BIO-WEAPONS and TYRANID PRIME units from your army have the following ability: Hive Predators: Each time a model in this unit makes an attack that targets a CHARACTER unit, you can re-roll the Hit roll.
